## ❌ What's Not Allowed

### 1. Violence & Threats
**Prohibited:**
- Threats of violence against individuals or groups
- Incitement to violence or harm
- Glorification of violence
- Graphic violent content
- Terrorism or extremist content

**Examples:**
- ❌ "I'm going to hurt you"
- ❌ "Let's attack [group]"
- ❌ Sharing violent imagery

### 2. Self-Harm & Suicide
**Prohibited:**
- Promotion or encouragement of self-harm
- Suicide instructions or methods
- Glorification of eating disorders
- Content that may trigger vulnerable users

**What We Do:**
- Provide crisis resources immediately
- Remove harmful content
- May contact emergency services if imminent danger

**If You're Struggling:**
- 🆘 **US**: 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline)
- 🆘 **India**: +91-9820466726 (AASRA)
- 🆘 **International**: befrienders.org

### 3. Harassment & Bullying
**Prohibited:**
- Targeted harassment of individuals
- Bullying or intimidation
- Doxxing (sharing private information)
- Stalking or unwanted contact
- Coordinated harassment campaigns

**Examples:**
- ❌ Repeatedly messaging someone who asked you to stop
- ❌ Sharing someone's personal information
- ❌ Organizing attacks against users

**Note:** Friendly banter and jokes between consenting users are okay, but stop if asked.

### 4. Hate Speech & Discrimination
**Prohibited:**
- Hate speech based on:
  - Race, ethnicity, national origin
  - Religion or beliefs
  - Gender or gender identity
  - Sexual orientation
  - Disability or medical condition
  - Age
- Slurs and derogatory language
- Promotion of hate groups
- Discrimination or exclusion

**Examples:**
- ❌ Racial slurs or stereotypes
- ❌ Transphobic or homophobic content
- ❌ Religious intolerance

### 5. Sexual Content
**Prohibited:**
- Sexually explicit content
- Pornography or nudity
- Sexual solicitation
- Sharing intimate images without consent
- Sexual content involving minors (zero tolerance)

**Examples:**
- ❌ Explicit images or videos
- ❌ Sexual propositions
- ❌ Unsolicited sexual messages

### 6. Spam & Manipulation
**Prohibited:**
- Spam or repetitive content
- Phishing or scams
- Malware or malicious links
- Fake accounts or impersonation
- Vote manipulation
- Automated bots (without permission)

**Examples:**
- ❌ Posting the same message repeatedly
- ❌ "Click here to win" scams
- ❌ Pretending to be someone else

### 7. Illegal Activity
**Prohibited:**
- Illegal goods or services
- Drug sales or promotion
- Weapons sales
- Human trafficking
- Copyright infringement
- Any violation of local laws

**Examples:**
- ❌ Selling drugs or weapons
- ❌ Sharing pirated content
- ❌ Promoting illegal activities

### 8. Misinformation
**Prohibited:**
- Dangerous health misinformation
- Election interference
- Manipulated media (deepfakes)
- Impersonation of officials
- Crisis misinformation

**Examples:**
- ❌ False medical advice
- ❌ Fake emergency alerts
- ❌ Manipulated videos

## 🛡️ Enforcement

### How We Moderate

1. **Automated Detection**: AI scans content for violations
2. **User Reports**: Community members flag content
3. **Human Review**: Moderators review flagged content
4. **Action Taken**: Appropriate consequences applied

### Consequences

Violations may result in:

**First Offense (Minor)**
- Warning message
- Content removal
- Temporary restrictions

**Repeat Offenses**
- Shadowban (messages hidden from others)
- Temporary suspension (24 hours - 7 days)
- Permanent account ban

**Severe Violations**
- Immediate permanent ban
- Report to law enforcement (if illegal)
- IP ban (for extreme cases)

### Heat System

We use a "heat system" for repeat offenders:
- **Clean**: No violations
- **Warm**: 1 violation - warning
- **Hot**: 2-3 violations - longer penalties
- **Burning**: 4-5 violations - severe restrictions
- **Inferno**: 6+ violations - permanent ban likely

Good behavior over time reduces your heat level.

## 🔄 Appeals Process

### If Your Content Was Removed

1. You'll receive a notification explaining why
2. You can appeal within 7 days
3. Click "Appeal" in the notification
4. Explain why you believe it was a mistake
5. We'll review within 48 hours

### Appeal Outcomes

- **Upheld**: Original decision stands
- **Overturned**: Content restored, record cleared
- **Modified**: Partial reversal or reduced penalty
